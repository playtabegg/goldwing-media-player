# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — September 2026

Goldwing Media Player for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

- Rialto movie/music disc playback and game disc launcher/menu support.
- Unprotected DVD-Video playback with supported menus and chapters.
- Unprotected Blu-ray playback with supported HDMV menus and chapters.
- Audio CDs, supported disc images/folders, and data-disc browsing.
- Disc metadata and transparent case/disc product renders.
- Keyboard, mouse and gamepad controls for supported disc navigation.
- Manual update checks with signed feed, installer and hash verification.
- Per-user installer signed by We The Indies, LLC; no file associations.

CSS-protected DVDs, AACS-protected Blu-rays and Java-based BD-J menus are not
included. Officially licensed protected-disc playback is planned, subject to
licensing, compliance and a future update; no date is promised.
